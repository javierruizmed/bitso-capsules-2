This capsule covers Access to my account — the single largest contact type in CS, representing 23–28% of total volume. The focus is on Issues with SMS and I can't restore my password, two sub-categories that were near-zero before March 2026 and have since become the fastest-growing drivers of friction at Bitso. Both trace directly to authentication infrastructure changes introduced in mid-March, and by April, SMS issues alone represented 36% of all Access to my account contacts.
01Incoming Overview

Access to my account is consistently the #1 contact type in CS, representing between 23% and 28% of all incoming tickets. Over the six-month period, it grew faster than total CS volume — increasing 47% from December to April, while overall CS incoming grew 20% in the same window.

02User Experience

Metric definitions

CSI — Customer Satisfaction Index
Survey that measures the experience of the user with Bitso on a scale from 1 to 10. Sent once the ticket is set to solved.

Score: Detractor (0–6) = −1  ·  Passive (7–8) = 0  ·  Promoter (9–10) = +1
(Promoters − Detractors) / Total surveys responded
CSAT — Customer Satisfaction
Survey that measures the experience of the user with the CS agent on a scale of 👍 / passive / 👎. Sent once the ticket is set to solved.

Score: Promoter = 3  ·  Passive = 2  ·  Detractor = 1
Total promoter surveys / Total surveys responded

03Voice of the Customer

Ticket and survey analysis surfaces consistent patterns across promoters and detractors. The quotes below come directly from customer feedback in the review period.

👍 What promoters say — they praise agents, but flag the product gap

Promoters acknowledge the underlying SMS problem but credit the support team for resolving it. However, even satisfied customers surface improvement opportunities:

"No me llegó el SMS para iniciar sesión y tuve que pedir ayuda a soporte, y el servicio fue muy bueno" — then adds: "deberían activar la función de que el código llegue al correo registrado." #3253587

Several promoters explicitly praised Bitso's security posture: "tienen un exigente sistema de seguridad, me siento confiado" #3203694 — yet a notable outlier scored positively overall despite writing: "tiene problemas con los SMS, y esa experiencia me es desagradable." #3261960 This suggests CSI is capturing the overall relationship with Bitso, not just the SMS resolution quality — which may be inflating scores.

👎 What detractors say — three recurring pain points

1. No SMS + no resolution path: "1: no da los números clave. 2: no dan solución" #3255311 — Users feel agents cannot resolve the SMS issue and offer no workaround. This is the most damaging pattern: the contact type generates high volume and the resolution rate is perceived as low.

2. 2FA confusion + carrier escalation needed: "La forma como indican el paso a paso de la verificación 2FA es muy confusa... y muy importante que hablen con los operadores de celular para que el mensaje de texto de verificación funcione." #3243664 Customers are asking Bitso to escalate directly with mobile carriers — they've already ruled out their own device as the cause.

3. Account blocks and frozen funds: "Dejar tanta burocracia y bloquear el dinero contra la voluntad del cliente viola los derechos individuales" #3265370 and "Te desconectan y no puedes hacer una operación." #3256168 SMS failures compound into account access blocks, which users perceive as Bitso holding their funds — the highest-stakes outcome.

From March detractors — process failures: One customer described following all technical recommendations, verifying that SMS from other senders arrived normally, speaking with at least 3 agents, and having a verification link expire in 2 minutes. Key quote: "Deben mejorar las opciones y atención... Ineficiente e inconveniente." #3212214

Alternative channel requests: "Estaría mejor por WhatsApp, como lo hacen algunas instituciones bancarias" #3207171 — users are actively proposing solutions, which indicates strong unmet need.

From February detractors — resolution time: "Todo — no es posible que llevo más de 72 HRS y nadie me escucha para poder ingresar a mi cuenta" #3151712 and "Muy molesto, 6 asesores y no me pudieron ayudar. Hablamos de dinero amigos." #3169958

04Root Cause — What Happened in March

The spike in SMS and password-related tickets is not random — it traces directly to a sequence of authentication changes and incidents that began in mid-March 2026. The timeline below reconstructs the causal chain.

12 Mar
SMS activated as validation channel Release
Bitso enables SMS as an authentication channel. This is the starting point — from this date, users begin relying on SMS codes for login and verification steps.
15 Mar
Core Authentication Server Scheduled Maintenance Maintenance
Planned downtime on the central authentication server. Any degradation here directly affects login, 2FA, and password restore flows.
16 Mar
Security StepUp required to configure 2FA Release
A new security step is now required to set up or change 2FA methods. This immediately introduced additional friction for users trying to access or recover their accounts.
24 Mar
First confirmed incident: SMS not delivered + 2FA issues Incident
Users report not receiving SMS codes. Simultaneously, 2FA issues are flagged. Password restore flow also affected — since it relies on SMS for identity verification.
26–27 Mar
Two consecutive days: SMS delivery failure Incident
"Users are not receiving the SMS to validate their phone number" — documented two days in a row. This is the event that drove the March ticket spike and set the trajectory toward the April peak.
16 Apr
Core Authentication Server Scheduled Maintenance Maintenance
Second maintenance event on the authentication server within a month. Coincides with the April volume peak.
22 Apr
Users unable to log in on mobile — 404 error Incident
Confirmed login failure on mobile. Direct impact on access tickets — users could not enter the app.
30 Apr
Hubble 2FA UI inconsistency Incident
Inconsistency in the 2FA interface detected in the internal tooling. Minor in scope but indicative of ongoing instability in auth flows.
ℹ️ On the "Forgot my password" → "I can't restore my password" shift: The historical "Forgot my password" category (1,415–1,800 tickets/month Dec–Feb) dropped to zero in April. At the same time, "I can't restore my password" — which had 1–5 tickets/month — exploded to 1,595. This strongly suggests a re-categorization of the contact reason in March, not an actual resolution of password issues. The underlying problem persists under a different label.
05Pain Points
🔴 SMS delivery failures blocking access and password recovery

The activation of SMS as an authentication channel on March 12 introduced a critical dependency: users who don't receive the SMS code cannot log in, cannot restore their password, and cannot complete 2FA setup. When delivery failed on March 24–27, this became a widespread blocker affecting thousands of users simultaneously.

The compounding effect is significant: Issues with SMS grew from ~580/month to 2,565 in April (+342%), and I can't restore my password went from near-zero to 1,595 (+31,800%) in the same period. Both categories are symptoms of the same root cause — SMS dependency without a reliable fallback for users when delivery fails.

⚠️ May 2026 data (partial, ~first week) already shows 545 SMS tickets and 291 password restore tickets. The issue has not fully resolved — volume remains elevated above the Dec–Feb baseline.
🟠 2FA friction added without sufficient fallback paths

The March 16 release requiring a Security StepUp to configure 2FA added friction for users who were already struggling with SMS delivery. Users trying to recover account access hit a wall: they need SMS to proceed, but SMS isn't arriving. The 2FA disable request category (I want to disable 2FA) remained stable at ~900–1,100/month throughout the period, suggesting this is a persistent structural friction independent of the March spike.

The April 7 "Liveness for enabling 2FA" release and the Hubble 2FA UI inconsistency on April 30 indicate that 2FA-related changes continued to be deployed in a period of already elevated user friction — potentially compounding the impact.

🟠 Mobile login outage (April 22) hit an already stressed contact type

The April 22 incident — users unable to log in on mobile due to a 404 error — landed on top of an already elevated ticket volume in Access to my account. April was already the peak month before this incident. Events like this, occurring when the category is already under stress, disproportionately impact CSI because there is no resolution path for the user within CS — the fix is purely on the product/engineering side.
