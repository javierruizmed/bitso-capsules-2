When a user sends crypto to an address on an incompatible blockchain, the funds become inaccessible. There is no validation in the app that prevents the transaction before it happens, and no self-service path to recover funds afterward. Every cross-chain case reaches the Rocket Rescue team — and when the recovery process is manual, it is long and entirely dependent on internal escalation. This capsule documents what that experience looks like for users, and where the gaps are.
01Incoming Overview

Cross-chain transactions generate a consistent volume of contacts to the Rocket Rescue team — between 66 and 115 tickets per month from January to May. April was the peak at 112 tickets. What the breakdown by process reveals is more telling than the total: the vast majority of cases go through the manual path, and the automated tool's share has been shrinking steadily.

03How the Recovery Processes Work

When a cross-chain transaction reaches the Rocket Rescue team, the outcome depends on whether the case falls within the scope of Bitso's automated recovery tool. The two paths are meaningfully different — both in what's required from the user and in how long recovery takes.

Path 1 — Automated Tool
1
User contacts CS, case is escalated to the Rocket Rescue team
2
The team checks if the asset/network combination is within scope
3
User accepts a 5% fee (max $25 USD), deducted from recovered funds
4
Recovery executes — funds deposited back to user's Bitso account
⏱ SLA: 2 hours – 3 days
Path 2 — Manual Escalation
1
User provides: incorrect hash, external non-Bitso wallet address, $20 USDC fee payment
2
Case escalates to Ops Cripto for review
3
Policy team approves
4
The Rocket Rescue team executes recovery via Fireblocks — funds sent to external wallet
⏱ SLA: 5 weeks (practice: up to 6+)
ℹ️ The user doesn't know in advance which path applies to their case. Both paths start with the same CS contact — only after escalation does the team determine whether the automated tool can handle it. For users who end up on Path 2, the SLA is communicated only after they've already paid the fee.
04Ticket Review — Three Cases, Two Paths

Three tickets from May 2026 illustrate the two recovery paths and the gap between them. Together they show what the process looks like from the user's side — including where communication breaks down.

✅ Path 1 — Automated tool, resolved in 1 day

User sent USDT via Arbitrum to a USDC address on Bitso. The Rocket Rescue team identified the case as within scope for the automated tool and offered recovery with a 5% fee deducted from the funds. User confirmed the same day. Funds were back in the Bitso account within hours. #3344745

This is the best-case scenario: the right tool, the right network, a same-day outcome. The user left satisfied.

🟠 Path 2 — Manual, resolved in 32 days with inconsistent communication

User sent USDT cross-chain in late April. The first message from Bitso said the recovery couldn't be done. Two days later, a second email said it could — creating confusion about whether the offer was legitimate. The user paid the $20 USDC fee on May 2. The SLA was originally communicated as 4 weeks; it was extended to 5 weeks at the time of payment, . The user followed up on June 1 when the 5 weeks passed with no news. Funds were recovered on June 3 — 32 days after payment. #3292325

The interaction resolved within the SLA, but only because the user tracked it actively. The SLA change at payment — from 4 to 5 weeks — added friction.

🔴 Path 2 — Manual, SLA exceeded, funds not yet recovered

User sent ETH via MegaETH (an unsupported L2) in early May. Initial response from the Rocket Rescue team said recovery wasn't possible at the time. Four days later, the case was reopened and the manual recovery path was offered. The user paid the $20 USDC fee on May 6. The team confirmed receipt and communicated a 5-week SLA. As of June 18 — 43 days since fee payment — the funds have not been returned. #3295885

This case is currently outside the communicated SLA. The user paid the fee, provided all required information, and has received no resolution. This is the highest-friction scenario in the current process.

05What Users Are Telling Us — And What Could Help

The low satisfaction scores for cross-chain aren't a reflection of poor agent performance — the tickets reviewed show agents handling the interactions professionally and with clear communication. The frustration comes from the process itself: weeks of waiting and a 5-week SLA that users only learn about after paying a fee.

Bitso already has the infrastructure to resolve these cases — the automated tool exists and it works. The opportunity is in expanding its coverage and improving the manual path's communication. Beyond the user experience, there is a concrete business case for doing so.

The business case for expanding coverage

The MOUFTCO May 2026 deck quantified what expanding the automated tool would mean in practice. Of the 3,357 cross-chain transactions identified in the period, 21.1% were resolved by the automated tool and 64.3% — 2,158 transactions — remain unresolved. Adding BSC (Binance Smart Chain) alone would resolve an estimated 350 additional transactions, generating $6,213+ USD in revenue and managing $2.5M+ in asset volume.

📋 Expand the automated tool's coverage

The difference between Path 1 and Path 2 is dramatic: same error, same user profile, but one resolves in hours and the other in weeks. Expanding the automated tool to cover additional blockchains and assets — starting with Binance Smart Chain — would shift more cases to Path 1 and reduce the manual load on the Rocket Rescue team. Estimated resolution time for converted cases: 2 hours to 3 days.

📋 Proactive communication during Path 2

Both manual cases reviewed showed users following up because they had no news. A simple status update at the 2-week and 4-week marks — even a brief "your case is in process, no action needed" — would reduce follow-up contacts and improve satisfaction without changing the recovery timeline itself.

📋 Communicate the SLA before the fee payment

In the cases reviewed, users learned the 5-week SLA only after paying the $20 USDC fee. In one case, the SLA was extended from 4 to 5 weeks at the moment of payment. Users should know the expected timeline before committing — this is a basic expectation for a paid service.

📋 Rocket Rescue team as end-to-end owner

The Rocket Rescue team is currently expanding its Fireblocks knowledge through onboarding with Ops Cripto. Once complete, the team would own the full Path 2 process end-to-end, removing the intermediate escalation steps. Estimated impact: reduction from 5 weeks to 1 week. This is the highest-leverage structural improvement available without changing the product.
